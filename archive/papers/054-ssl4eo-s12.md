# SSL4EO-S12: A Large-Scale Multi-Modal, Multi-Temporal Dataset for Self-Supervised Learning in Earth Observation

## Problem
Self-supervised learning in Earth observation lacked a large, open, global dataset covering both Sentinel-1 radar and Sentinel-2 optical data across seasons. Without one, pretrained models were hard to compare and often trained on ImageNet-style RGB.

## Approach
The authors sample about 251,000 locations worldwide and, for each, collect four seasonal acquisitions of Sentinel-1 dual-polarisation SAR, Sentinel-2 L1C (top of atmosphere) and Sentinel-2 L2A (surface reflectance), as patches about 2.64 km on a side. They then pretrain with four self-supervised methods: MoCo-v2, DINO, MAE and data2vec.

## Data and benchmarks
- About 1.5 TB raw (roughly 500 GB per modality), with compressed 8-bit versions and a small example subset.
- Pretrained backbones: ResNet50, ViT-S/16, ViT-B/16 and ViT-L/16 for S1 (2 bands), S2 (13 bands) and RGB.
- Downstream: scene classification, semantic segmentation and change detection on standard Sentinel benchmarks.
- Licence: code Apache 2.0, dataset and models CC-BY-4.0; weights also available in TorchGeo.

## Key results
Per the abstract, pretraining on SSL4EO-S12 gives downstream accuracy close to, or above, supervised learning, and outperforms existing pretraining datasets. We did not re-verify individual scores.

## Limitations
- Patches are seasonal snapshots, not dense time series, so short events like floods are rarely captured.
- Locations are sampled around populated areas; Indian rural cropland coverage is not quantified.
- Backbones give features only; a labelled head or a probe is needed for each task.
- A later v1.1 update (arXiv 2503.00168) exists; we did not review it here.

## What it means for SatClip
- Adopt the small SSL4EO-S12 backbones (ResNet50 or ViT-S/16 via TorchGeo) for CPU experiments where CLIP struggles, such as Sentinel-1 patches, since CLIP never saw SAR.
- Use a linear probe on these features as a land cover cross-check: when the probe and zero-shot CLIP disagree, lower confidence or abstain.
- Use SSL4EO-S12 patches (with the S2 L2A product, which matches what we read from STAC) as an unlabelled pool for calibration splits and for generating synthetic question-answer pairs for the LoRA pipeline.
- CC-BY-4.0 means attribution must appear in our model card and README.
