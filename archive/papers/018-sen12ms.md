# SEN12MS: A Curated Dataset of Georeferenced Multi-Spectral Sentinel-1/2 Imagery for Deep Learning and Data Fusion

## Problem
In 2019, most remote sensing deep learning datasets were small, single-sensor, regionally limited or not georeferenced. SAR-optical fusion research in particular lacked a large, global, openly licensed training set.

## Approach
The authors used Google Earth Engine to pull freely available Copernicus data and assemble co-registered triplets: a dual-polarisation Sentinel-1 SAR patch, a multispectral Sentinel-2 patch, and a MODIS land-cover map patch. All patches are georeferenced, resampled to 10 m, and sampled across every inhabited continent and all four meteorological seasons. The GitHub repository adds train/test splits, single-label and multi-label scene labels derived from the land-cover maps, and baseline ResNet and DenseNet classifiers with pretrained weights.

## Data and benchmarks
- 180,662 triplets (541,986 patches), 256x256 px at 10 m.
- Land-cover labels follow the IGBP scheme, with a simplified IGBP variant used in follow-up work.
- Data on mediaTUM: https://mediatum.ub.tum.de/1474000.
- Exact number of regions and per-band details were not confirmed on the fetched pages.

## Key results
This is a dataset paper. The abstract and README fetched do not report headline accuracy numbers, so none are given here.

## Limitations
MODIS land cover is coarse (500 m native), so labels at 10 m are noisy. Sentinel-2 patches are mosaics, so they are not single dated observations. Coverage is global but sampled sparsely, so district-level Indian variety is thin.

## What it means for SatClip
- SEN12MS is a ready source of paired S1/S2 data for teaching a model that SAR and optical views of one place mean the same thing, useful for monsoon months when optical is clouded.
- Use the Earth Engine sampling recipe as a template for building a small Indian S1/S2 paired set from live STAC data.
- Do not treat its MODIS-derived labels as truth for fine-grained answers; use them only for coarse pretraining or weak supervision.
- Keep georeferencing end to end, as SEN12MS does, so every SatClip answer can cite scene ID, date and footprint.
- Baseline ResNet weights from the repo can serve as a cheap CPU land-cover head to cross-check VLM claims.
